# Design assets

Source material and past design work. **Not part of the deployed site.**

These lived in `public/images/assets/` until September 2026, which meant every file
shipped to Vercel and was publicly fetchable at `cosmonus.com/images/assets/...` —
including client brand marks. Nothing on the site ever referenced them.

Anything that needs to be served by the site belongs in `public/`. `public/` now holds
only three files: the founder photo, the wordmark used by the header and footer mask,
and the logo referenced by the Organization JSON-LD.
